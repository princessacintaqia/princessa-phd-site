Welcome

I'm Princessa Cintaqia. I'm a PhD Candidate at Boston University's Faculty of Computing and Data Sciences, advised by Dr. Allison McDonald. I'm interested in Computer Security and Privacy, specifically in anything related to Digital Intimacy.

I previously did research at Rutgers University, the Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy, and Penn State University. I earned my bachelor's degree from Universitas Indonesia.
